Bawana Election Dashboard

EXPECTED MCD ELECTION — FEB 2027
Read of the race: BJP enters 2027 with a comfortable assembly margin but zero MCD-level track record here — its 2025 win hasn't yet been tested at the ward level, where the campaign will actually be fought. AAP lost the seat by double digits but keeps a deeper organisational base built over a decade of SC-reserved-seat wins, and it's already campaigning early. INC remains a distant third but is the only party gaining ground, making it a longer-term rebuild rather than a 2027 contender. The most persuadable segments — first-time voters and industrial workers — are also the ones least locked into either major party, making them the real battleground here.

Where Each Party Stands

BJP ▾

  • Won the seat in 2025 with a 31,475-vote margin
  • Holds the MCD mayor's post citywide
  • First civic-level test since the assembly win
Strategy note: needs to convert assembly-level goodwill into ward-level delivery credit before 2027 — incumbency now cuts both ways.

AAP ▾

  • Held this seat 2015–2025, deep prior organisation
  • Won all 12 Delhi SC-reserved seats in 2020
  • Already campaigning for 2027, over 2 years out
Strategy note: needs to re-energise cadre after a demoralising assembly loss, likely by leaning hard on local civic delivery promises.

INC ▾

  • Only party gaining vote share in 2025 (+1.7pp)
  • Held the seat 1998–2013, long institutional memory
  • Starting from under 10% — needs a local, not state, strategy
Strategy note: a serious ward win here needs hyper-local candidates and issues, not a state-level Congress message.
